How It Works

  1. Upload your CSV file with player codes and coach/team names
  2. Preview the matches - the system will show which players will be assigned to which teams
  3. Review any errors (missing players, unknown coaches)
  4. Confirm to execute the batch assignment

Player Matching

Players are matched by their player code (e.g., "bennluk1810"). This is the unique identifier assigned during registration import.

Team Matching

Teams are matched by coach name. The system will try to match:

  • Full coach name (e.g., "John Smith")
  • Last name only (e.g., "Smith")
