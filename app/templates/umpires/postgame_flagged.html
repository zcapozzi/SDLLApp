About Flagged Reports

Reports are automatically flagged when a coach's rating is significantly different from their historical average (2+ levels deviation). This helps identify:

  • Potentially biased feedback
  • Genuinely exceptional or problematic umpire performance
  • Data entry errors

After reviewing a flagged report, click "Clear Flag" to acknowledge it has been reviewed. The underlying feedback data remains unchanged.
