About Weekly Digests

  • Automatic Generation: Digests are auto-generated every Sunday at 6pm ET
  • Review Mode: By default, digests require manual approval before sending
  • Auto-Send Mode: Enable per-partner in Settings to skip review and send immediately
  • Reminders: If digests aren't sent by Monday 8am, admins receive a reminder
  • No Games: Partners with zero games for the week are automatically skipped (no email sent)
