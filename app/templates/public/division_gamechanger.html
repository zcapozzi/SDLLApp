About This Tool

This tool helps you upload your division's game schedule to GameChanger. GameChanger requires that teams exist in the system before games can be imported, so we'll guide you through the process step by step.

Important: You must complete each step in order. The CSV download will only be available once all teams have their GameChanger names configured.

Need help? If you get stuck at any point, there's a help form at the bottom of this page where you can send us a message.

1

Create League in GameChanger

First, you need to create a league in GameChanger (or find a previously created league for your division's season) and add your division's teams to it.

  • Open the GameChanger app on your device
  • Create a new league for {{ display_name }}, or find the existing league if one was already created for this season
  • Add each team in your division to the league
  • Important: Do NOT manually create teams. Search for existing GameChanger teams and add them to your league.

If you need to manage your GameChanger account online, look for the "Manage Online" option in the app, which will send a link to your email.

2

Enter GameChanger Team Names

Enter the exact team names as they appear in GameChanger. These names must match exactly for the schedule import to work.

For example, a team might be listed in SDLL as "Brown" but in GameChanger as "SDLL Cactus Brown Orange Fall 2026".

5

Upload to GameChanger

Once you have the CSV file:

  1. Go to your GameChanger league online (use the "Manage Online" link from the app)
  2. Navigate to Schedule
  3. Click Add Games and select Import schedule from template
  4. Click Select CSV and choose the file you downloaded
  5. Review the games - they should appear in green if valid
  6. Click Import Schedule

Troubleshooting: If you see errors, the most common issue is:

  • Team name mismatch: The GameChanger name doesn't match exactly. Go back to Step 2 and correct it. Names must match exactly, including capitalization and spacing.
