Privacy Policy

South Durham Little League - How we handle your data

The Short Version: We don't collect personal information. We don't share data with third parties. We don't track you across the web.

What We Collect

When you visit our public schedule pages, we collect anonymous usage data to help us understand how the site is being used:

Data Purpose Privacy Protection
Page visits Know which schedules are popular Not linked to identity
Device type Optimize for mobile vs desktop General category only
Time on page Measure engagement Anonymous aggregate
IP address Geographic analysis Hashed immediately - we cannot reverse it

What We Don't Collect

  • Names or email addresses - We don't ask for them
  • Personal information about children - Our public pages don't collect any PII
  • Your browsing history - We only know you visited our site
  • Anything that identifies you personally

No Third-Party Tracking

Unlike most websites, we do not use:

  • Google Analytics
  • Facebook Pixel
  • Any advertising networks
  • Any third-party tracking services

All data stays on our servers. We never share or sell data to anyone.

Cookies

We use a single cookie to give you an anonymous session ID. This cookie:

  • Contains a random string (like "abc123xyz")
  • Is not linked to your identity in any way
  • Only works on our website (first-party)
  • Expires after 30 days
  • Helps us count unique visitors vs. repeat visits

Sponsor Content

Our schedule pages may display sponsor messages or announcements from SDLL. These are:

  • Hosted entirely on our servers (no ad networks)
  • Not targeted based on your personal information
  • Tracked only for aggregate reporting (total views, clicks)

Children's Privacy

We take extra care because Little League involves children:

  • We do not collect personal information from any visitor
  • We do not require accounts to view schedules
  • Admin accounts are for adults only

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal information, there's nothing to delete or export. If you have questions or concerns, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this policy, we'll update this page. Our commitment to privacy-first practices won't change.
