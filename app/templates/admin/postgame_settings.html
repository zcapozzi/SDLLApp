How Post-Game Reports Work

  1. Email Trigger: 105 minutes after a game's scheduled start time, emails are sent to coaches
  2. Form Submission: Head coach receives email (assistants CC'd) with a secure link
  3. Report Contents: Coaches submit final score, innings played, and umpire evaluation
  4. Score Reconciliation: When both teams report matching scores, the game is marked complete
  5. Edit Window: Coaches can edit their report within 24 hours of submission
