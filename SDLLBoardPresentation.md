# SDLL Web Application: Board Member Overview

## Why We Built This

South Durham Little League has grown significantly over the years, and with that growth came increasing complexity in managing schedules, fields, umpires, coaches, and communications. Like many volunteer-run organizations, we relied on a patchwork of spreadsheets, email threads, Google Docs, and manual processes that were:

- **Time-consuming** - Board members spent hours on administrative tasks that could be automated
- **Error-prone** - Manual data entry led to scheduling conflicts, missed communications, and frustrated families
- **Knowledge-dependent** - When a board member rotated off, institutional knowledge was often lost
- **Disconnected** - Different board roles used different tools, making coordination difficult

The SDLL Web Application was created to centralize league operations into a single, purpose-built platform. Our goals were to:

1. **Reduce volunteer workload** by automating repetitive tasks
2. **Improve accuracy** through validation and conflict detection
3. **Preserve institutional knowledge** in a persistent system
4. **Enable self-service** so coaches, umpires, and families can find information without board intervention
5. **Provide visibility** so board members can see the full picture of league operations

## How It's Going

The application has been in active development and use, with significant functionality already deployed:

- **Scheduling System** - Full season schedule generation, conflict detection, and management
- **Field Management** - Comprehensive field properties, allocations, and time restrictions
- **Umpire Coordination** - Assignment tracking, partner integration (Assignr), and payment management
- **Public Schedules** - Shareable team and division schedules with GameChanger integration
- **User Management** - Role-based access control for all board positions
- **Facilities Management** - Field captain assignments and field schedule viewing
- **Analytics** - Usage tracking to understand how families interact with the system

The system currently supports multiple seasons, both baseball and softball, and handles hundreds of games per season.

## Board Role Capabilities

### President / President-elect / Immediate Past President

**Current Capabilities:**
- Full administrative access to all system features (when granted admin role)
- Master Schedule view showing all league activities
- User management for all board members and volunteers
- Season creation and configuration
- Access to all reports and audit trails

**Potential Enhancements:**
- *Board Dashboard* - Executive summary of league health metrics (enrollment trends, volunteer counts, budget status)
- *Board Meeting Prep* - Automated reports summarizing activity since last meeting
- *Succession Planning Tools* - Document and video library for board role transitions
- *Policy Repository* - Centralized storage for bylaws, policies, and procedures
- *Communication Hub* - Unified messaging to all members or filtered groups
